## What this changes in the plan

1. **Aguinaldo and salary calculators beat an IVA calculator.** ~5 000/mo combined for aguinaldo phrasings plus 5 400 for "liquidacion de salario". Phase B3 gets `calculadora de aguinaldo` and `liquidación de salario / finiquito` as tools 1 and 2; IVA calculator stays but is third priority.
2. **Marangatu and Ekuatia are navigational giants.** People want the DNIT login and how-tos. `/marangatu/` and `/ekuatia/` keep their service framing but gain a "Guía" block (cómo ingresar, recuperar clave, errores frecuentes, CCT) to catch long-tail informational traffic. Never bid on the bare terms in Ads (98 kr top bid for login intent is waste).
3. **IRP has no page.** 1 600/mo, zero coverage on the old site. Add `/irp/` (Impuesto a la Renta Personal) as a new service page in B1, cluster Gestión empresarial.
4. **Certificado de Cumplimiento Tributario is the fastest grower** (+136 %). Dedicated section on `/marangatu/` with an H2 that matches the phrase, plus a blog article "Cómo obtener el Certificado de Cumplimiento Tributario".
5. **Formulario 120 / IRE general / IRE simple** cluster on `/ire-simple/` (H2s for each) plus the launch article "IRE Simple vs Resimple vs IRE General".
6. **Financial-statement terms** (balance general, estados financieros, estado de resultados, flujo de efectivo) support the new `/contabilidad/` page and one explainer article.
7. **Ads shortlist, exact/phrase:** estudio contable, factura electrónica paraguay (+ variants), sifen, inscripción ruc, liquidacion de salario, ire simple, irp paraguay, certificado cumplimiento tributario, multas dnit, auditoría interna. Landing pages exist for all of them after B1.
8. **Low-volume commercial terms** (cambiar de contador, honorarios contador, contabilidad para pymes) still get pages/sections because they convert, but they are not Ads targets.
